# I Need a Hero – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

**I Need a Hero collects nothing about you.**

## Everything stays on your device

The app makes no network requests of its own. The public data it draws people from is built into the
app. The people you roll, save and annotate are stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes
them.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer cannot see
who you roll or what you save.

## Portraits

If you choose Image Playground portraits, the picture is made by Apple Intelligence from a short
description of the person's looks. Apple's handling of these requests is described in Apple's own privacy
policy. The sketch and illustrated portraits are drawn by the app itself.

## Sharing

When you share a character sheet, the app creates a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes
from there is your choice.

## Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details and
no personal information.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

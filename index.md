# I Need a Hero

**Roll up a believable person for your story in one tap.**

I Need a Hero is for writers, game masters and anyone who needs a character in a hurry. Each roll is a
whole person: a name that fits their age and background, a job, a home town, how tall they are, what
they look like, a few quirks, and a short paragraph you can paste straight into your notes.

The odds come from public American data rather than guesswork: Social Security baby names, the
Census, CDC body measurements, the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Pew Religious Landscape Study.
Pick a state, county or town and the people you roll are drawn from the people who live there.

- **Roll a hero**, lock the traits you like, and roll again for the rest.
- **Roll a family** with ages that add up, or **cast a whole town**: the sheriff, the pastor, the
  waitress at the diner.
- **Keep your cast** on the shelf, with notes, and share them as Markdown or PDF character sheets.
- **A hero of the day** widget for your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Everything runs on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
